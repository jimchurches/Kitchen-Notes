# Pecan Pie

## Overview

A classic pecan pie with a rich golden syrup filling and a nutty pecan top.

This is the Australian-friendly version, using golden syrup instead of American corn syrup. The filling is sweet, buttery, and butterscotch-like, with pecans scattered into the pastry shell before baking. No need to arrange the nuts neatly; they float and settle into their own pattern.

Serve slightly warm or at room temperature with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.

Serves 6.

## Ingredients (metric/Australian)

- 1 quantity sour cream shortcrust pastry, prepared and blind baked
- 50 g unsalted butter, chopped
- 150 g brown sugar, firmly packed
- 160 ml golden syrup
- 3 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 240 g pecan halves
- Whipped cream or vanilla ice cream, to serve

## Oven temps and cooking temps when relevant

- Blind baking pastry shell: follow the sour cream shortcrust pastry recipe
- Final pie bake: 175°C
- Filling bake time: about 35 minutes

The pie is done when the edges are firm and the centre still has a slight wobble. It will continue to set as it cools.

## Instructions

1. Prepare one quantity of sour cream shortcrust pastry using the separate recipe in the repo.

2. Roll, line the pie tin, chill, and blind bake the pastry shell as directed in that recipe.

3. Reduce the oven to 175°C.

4. Place the butter, brown sugar, and golden syrup in a small saucepan.

5. Cook over low to medium heat, stirring, for about 5 minutes, or until the butter has melted and the mixture is smooth.

6. Remove from the heat and let the mixture cool slightly.

7. Add the beaten eggs and vanilla extract.

8. Whisk until combined.

9. Scatter the pecans evenly over the blind-baked pastry base.

10. Pour the golden syrup mixture over the pecans.

11. Place the pie tin on an oven tray.

12. Bake for about 35 minutes, or until the filling is browned and mostly firm to the touch.

13. Check doneness with a gentle jiggle. The edge should be set and the centre should move slightly.

14. Cool in the tin to room temperature before slicing.

15. Serve with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.

## Serving ideas and enhancements

- Serve warm in winter or at room temperature after a barbecue or lunch.
- Whipped cream keeps it simple.
- Vanilla ice cream makes it more dessert-like.
- A small splash of bourbon or dark rum can be added to the filling for a richer version.
- Dark brown sugar gives a deeper molasses flavour; light brown sugar keeps the filling a little cleaner.
- Check that the pecans are fresh. Old pecans can taste rancid.
- Pecan pie can be made ahead. Cool completely, cover, and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
- To reheat, warm gently in a low oven for about 15 minutes.
- The baked pie can be frozen for longer storage, then thawed in the fridge and gently reheated.
