# Turkey Burgers

## Overview

Turkey mince can make excellent burgers, but it needs a little more help than beef. Because turkey is leaner and milder, the patties benefit from added moisture, seasoning, and a binder.

This version makes a proper burger rather than a health-food compromise: juicy turkey patties, cheddar, pickles, tomato, lettuce, onion, bacon, and a sharp creamy burger sauce. The result is lighter than a beef burger but still satisfying.

## Ingredients (metric/Australian)

### Patties

For about 4 burgers:

- 500 g turkey mince
- 1 small egg
- ½ cup breadcrumbs, panko, or crushed crackers
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise, Greek yoghurt, or olive oil
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard or American mustard
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce or soy sauce
- 1 small onion, grated, or ½ teaspoon onion powder
- 1 garlic clove, crushed, or ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and black pepper
- Oil, for cooking

### Burger sauce

- 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon tomato sauce
- 1 teaspoon American mustard or Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon pickle juice
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- Small splash Worcestershire sauce
- Black pepper

Optional:

- Finely chopped pickle

### To assemble

- Burger buns
- Cheddar cheese slices
- Pickles
- Tomato slices
- Lettuce
- Cooked bacon
- Cooked onion
- Butter, for toasting buns

## Oven temps and cooking temps when relevant

Turkey mince should be cooked through.

- Internal temperature: **74°C**
- Pan heat: **medium to medium-high**
- Avoid very high smash-burger heat, as turkey can brown or char before the centre is cooked.

## Instructions

1. Place the turkey mince in a bowl.

2. Add the egg, breadcrumbs, mayonnaise or yoghurt, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, onion, garlic, salt, and pepper.

3. Mix gently until combined. Do not overwork the mixture.

4. Shape into burger patties.

5. If time allows, chill the patties for 15–30 minutes to help them firm up.

6. Mix the burger sauce ingredients in a small bowl. Taste and adjust with more pickle juice, mustard, or pepper if needed.

7. Heat a frying pan over medium to medium-high heat with a little oil.

8. Cook the patties for about 3–4 minutes on the first side, until browned.

9. Flip once and cook for another 4–6 minutes, depending on thickness.

10. If the outside is browning too quickly, reduce the heat to medium and cover the pan briefly to help the centre cook through.

11. Add cheddar near the end of cooking and cover briefly to melt.

12. Rest the patties for 2–3 minutes.

13. Toast the buns in butter while the patties rest.

14. Assemble with sauce, lettuce, turkey patty, melted cheddar, bacon, onion, tomato, and pickles.

## Serving ideas and enhancements

- Keep the sauce sharp and creamy rather than too sweet. BBQ sauce can overpower turkey, especially with bacon and onion.
- Sauce both bun halves lightly so the burger stays moist without becoming sloppy.
- Put lettuce under the patty if the burger is juicy.
- Bacon and onion work well above the cheese.
- Keep pickles near the sauce so the acidity cuts through the richness.
- For a spicier version, add chilli, chipotle sauce, or cayenne to the burger sauce.
- For a Greek-style version, use oregano, garlic, lemon zest, feta, and yoghurt sauce.
- For a herby version, add parsley, sage, thyme, or chives to the patty mix.
